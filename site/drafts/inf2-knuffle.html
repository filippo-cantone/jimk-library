Knuffle Bunny — stop and infer

Book unitReading · InferringKnuffle Bunny · 15 minutes
Mo Willems’ Knuffle Bunny: Trixie, who has not yet learnt how to talk, loses her Knuffle Bunny at the laundromat — then speaks her first words. Stop at the listed points and infer from the pictures before reading on.

Steps

  1. 1Cover + pre-title — Look at cover and page prior to title page. Infer from pictures: What has happened before the story begins?
  2. 2Then they left — Read to Then they left. Inference: What will happen next?
  3. 3Aggle flaggle — Read to Trixie turned to her daddy and said, “Aggle flaggle klabble!” What would Trixie say if she could talk?
  4. 4Plot map — Track setting the scene, rising action, foreshadowing, climax, falling action, suspense, resolution.

Worked example: plot development map

StageIn Knuffle Bunny
Rising actionAt the Laundromat they leave Knuffle Bunny behind.
ForeshadowingKnuffle Bunny is in the wash basket and Dad doesn’t seem to be watching what he is loading.
ClimaxTrixie can’t get the message through to Dad.
ResolutionTrixie says her first words: “Knuffle Bunny.”

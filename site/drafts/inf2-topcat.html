Inferring guide / Top Cat

Top Cat

Book unitReading · InferringLois Ehlert · 15 minutes
Top Cat rules the roost — until a new cat moves in. Infer as you read why Top Cat won’t share, what the Swish swish tail means, and why he changes his mind.

Steps

  1. 1Title — What do you think Top Cat means?
  2. 2Sharing — Why does Top Cat think that one cat is enough? Why doesn’t Top Cat want to share?
  3. 3Tail talk — What is Top Cat doing when his tail goes Swish swish?
  4. 4Change + feelings — Why does Top Cat change his mind? How do the cats feel at the end — how can you tell? Themes: change, sharing.

Worked example: from hisses to purrs

“One cat’s enough … I don’t want to share my stuff. Swish swish … Go away, cat! Grrrr Hiss Hiss You’ve invaded my space.” By the end: “Welcome back! Let’s drink milk in our furs. No hisses, no scratches, no bites. Just purrs.” Vocabulary to pre-teach: pet, guard, invaded, message, boss, striped, leap, bounce, bare, company, decide.

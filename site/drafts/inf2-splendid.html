A Splendid Friend, Indeed — illustration inferences

Book lessonReading · InferringSuzanne Bloom · 10 minutes
Many inferences come from illustrations. Goose keeps interrupting Bear, who is trying to read and write alone — infer through the expressions on the polar bear’s face and guess what Bear might do next.

You will need A Splendid Friend, Indeed from the school library.

Steps

  1. 1Read + watch faces — Infer through the expressions on the face of the polar bear.
  2. 2Predict — Challenge students to guess what the Bear might do next.
  3. 3Name traits — Character traits: patience, friendship.
  4. 4Close — Bear is won over and finds Goose to be a splendid friend: “I like you, too. Indeed, I do. You are my splendid friend.”

Worked example: won over

Goose writes a note, reads it aloud — “I like you. Indeed I do. You are my splendid friend” — and Bear answers, “Thank you. I like you, too. Indeed, I do. You are my splendid friend. My splendid friend, indeed.” A very funny story with a warm message about not judging people on first impressions.

Using Illustrations: TC + BK = I

RoutineReading · InferringAny picture book · 10 minutes
Illustrations help students to infer and therefore enhance meaning. Merge text clues with background knowledge to construct the inference: TC + BK = I.

Steps

  1. 1Model the formula — model the inferring formula, demonstrating how you use the equation to construct meaning.
  2. 2Activate background knowledge — explain how you activate your background knowledge (e.g. about times when we're rushing off to work).
  3. 3Merge with text clues — merge it with clues from the text (e.g. that show how short the mum is with the boy).
  4. 4Anchor chart — add to anchor chart as the reading proceeds: Text Clues | Background Knowledge | Inference.

Worked example: "Not now, not again"

Text CluesBackground KnowledgeInference
The mum is short with him and says, "No, not now, not again," and tells him not to bother her when she's busy.It's sometimes hard to give children much time in the morning when parents are rushing to work.This is not the first time the boy has asked for a dog.
Turn and talk: use background knowledge about baby sitters and what they should be doing and merge it with clues from the text to infer why the father was mad.

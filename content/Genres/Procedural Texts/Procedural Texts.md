How to Play Pick Up Sticks
By an eHow Contributor
Pick Up Sticks is a great yet simple game that helps kids learn to count when scoring and
improves their manual dexterity. A typical Pick Up Sticks set contains 50 multi-colored sticks
made of wood, plastic or metal. All the sticks either have a number of rings around the center,
which indicates the point value or they are color coded. It is a simple game to play with
children of any age. Grandparents will especially love teaching their grandchildren how to play
this fun game.
Difficulty:Easy
Instructions
Things You'll Need
¢ Pick up sticks
1 Clear the area of play of any obstructions. A flat surface
such as a table or floor needs to be used.
2 Drop all the sticks onto the floor or table. Let the lie where
they landed. Do not rearrange any of the sticks.
3 Extract a stick from the pile without moving any other stick.
Take tums trying to remove the sticks carefully. If you
succeed, it remains your turn.
4 Forfeit your turn ifa stick moves. It does not matter how
slightly the stick moves.
5 Remove the stick that has been first touched. No other stick
can be removed until that specific one has been safely
removed.
6 Finish the game by picking up all the sticks from the pile
without disturbing the others.
7 Score the game by adding the point value of the sticks as
determined by the number of bands or colors indicated on ~
each stick.
8 Vary the game by adding a solid colored or black stick
which can then be used to assist in moving or picking up the
dropped sticks without penalty.

How to Play Hopscotch
By an eHow Contributor .
Do you remember playing outdoor games as a kid? Everyone should have a chance to learn
how to play Hopscotch. Children ages 3 and up can get involved in drawing the design and
learning the "hops." Grown-ups can test their balance and have fun too.
Difficulty:-Moderately Easy
Instructions
Things You'll Need
» Chall
» Small stone
Prepare to Play
1 Choose a flat space to play with enough room for 7 large
hops.
2 Draw the design using single boxes and side-by-side boxes a
little bigger than your feet.
3 Draw 3 single boxes on top of one another and number them
1, 2 and 3, with 1 closest to the start/ finish line.
4 Above square 3, draw two boxes side by side, numbered 4
and 5.
5 Draw a single box, 6. And above that, draw side-by-side
boxes 7 and 8. Draw a semicircle on top, labeled "Home."
Hopscotch Rules
1 Toss a stone on square 1 from the start/finish line. You will
hop over the stone on your way toward Home and pick it up
on your way back. Don't step on a square with a stone. If you
miss your toss, you lose your turn to the next player.
2 Hop to the first empty Hopscotch square. Use one foot to
reach a single box or both feet to arrive in side-by-side
boxes. Don't put two feet in a single box or step outside the
lines.
3 Hop toward Home (you may use both feet at Home to turn
around). Hop back, pausing at the square before your stone.
Bend over and pick up the stone without moving your feet.
Hop into that square and on toward the start/finish line.
4 Play until you are "out." If you lose your balance or put your
foot in the wrong place, you lose your turn to the next
player.
5 Continue Hopscotch play by tossing the stone in the next
numbered box and repeating the steps.

How to Play Texas Hopscotch
By an eHow Contributor
There are different types of hopscotch games. Texas hopscotch resembles the regular type of
hopscotch played in the United States with just a few variations. Following these steps will
show you how to play the game of hopscotch Texas style.
Difficulty:Easy
Instructions
Things You'll Need
» At least 2 players
» A puck
* Sidewalk chalk
° Sidewalk

Set Up

1 Find a puck to use in your game. A small lid from a bottle or

jar works great.

2 Draw two squares with sidewalk chalk side by side and
number them "1" and "2" from left to right.

3 Draw one square the same size above these two directly in
the middle and label it "3."

4 Draw a large box above the third square so that the width is
the same as the first two boxes put together and the length is
about one and a half as long as the boxes you've already
drawn.

5 Draw an "X" on this large box from corner to corner. You
should now have four triangles in the box. Label the closest
triangle to number three as "4", the next triangle to the left of
four label "5," the triangle to the right of number four label
"6," and the top triangle label "7."

6 Draw another single box identical to number three directly
above and in the middle of the top of the large square. Label
this box as "8."

7 Draw two squares above number eight identical to the first
two squares you drew in the grid. Label them "9" and "10"
from left to right. Now you have your Texas type hopscotch

grid and you're ready to play.

1 Throw your puck into the first square.

How to Play French Hopscotch
By an eHow Contributor
Kids all around the world play hopscotch. From country to country the name is different as
well as the way the hopscotch grid is drawn. The following steps will show you how to draw
and play the French version of hopscotch, which is called "Escargot" in French.
Instructions
Draw the Hopscotch Grid
1 Draw a large swirl on the sidewalk or pavement for French
hopscotch. The hopscotch will look like a large snail and
have 3 large swirls inside it with a small circle in the very
middle.
9) Make lines to form boxes all around the swirls. You should
have 16 lines when you are done.
3 Number the boxes starting at one on the bottom of the first
swirl on the outside and going all the way to 17 in the
middle.
Play the Hopscotch Game
1 Hop on one foot starting at square one all the way to the
middle of the swirl. Take a rest in every center of the
hopscotch grid.
2 Lose a turn if you step on any lines as you jump through the
hopscotch grid, causing the next person in line to start their
turn.
3 Return to square one by hopping all the way back around on
the same foot.
4 Go around again and back for the second time.
5 Choose a square on the way back for the second time to
mark as your "house" if you were successful in hopping in
and out both times without stepping on any lines. Draw your
initials on the square you have chosen.
6 Take turns going around the hopscotch grid hopping on one
foot, resting on the squares with your initials in them with
both feet and jumping over squares with other people's
initials on them. With each turn, choose an additional square
to write your initials on.
7 End the game when it is impossible for anyone to hop to the
center of the hopscotch grid. The winner is the player with
the most squares with their initials in them at the end of the
fame.

How to Play Chinese Jump Rope
By an eHow Contributor
Chinese Jump Rope is a children's game that has spread to worldwide popularity. It helps keep
kids active and teaches physical dexterity. More advanced than traditional jump rope, the game
uses a loop of elastic rope and requires two holders plus one jumper.
Difficulty:Moderate
Instructions
Things You'll Need
» Flat surface for jumping
» Chinese jump rope
+ 3 or more players
How to Play Chinese Jump Rope
] Select two rope holders and one player to begin. Holders
stand facing each other with the loop of elastic rope around
their ankles, at enough distance to pull the rope taut.
2 Demonstrate the jumps to any new players. "In" means both
feet inside the rope. "Out" means both feet outside the rope,
one on either side. "Side" means both feet outside the rope
on the same side, usually followed by a jump to the opposite
side. "On" means one foot on top of each side of the rope,
pressing them to the ground.
3 Chant the steps in order as the player jumps them: In, out,
side, side, on, in and out. For more advanced play, have one
of the holders chant a random series that the player must
follow.
4 Jump through the whole series of chants without missing and
move up to the next level, with more advanced jumps or the
ropes spread farther apart. Step on the rope at the wrong
time or jump in the wrong place and you must sit out a turn
in the player rotation.

Chinese Jump Rope Tricks
By Juliet Myfanwy Johnson, eHow Contributor
Everyone has seen a typical child's jump rope--a long rope with handles for turning over a
single jumper's head and under the jumper's feet in succession. Chinese jump rope is a little bit
different. That's because it is a long, continuous loop of a rope. It requires three children to
play. Chinese jump rope has been played in China for almost 1,600 years. Historically, Chinese
people called jump roping the game of "jumping one-hundred threads." Jump ropes could be
made out of many materials, including cloth covered elastic, ribbon and weaved grass. The
modern Chinese jump rope can be rope, plastic or elastic. It requires high jumping and
dexterity of the feet.
Difficulty:Easy
Instructions
Things You'll Need
+ Chinese jump rope (10 to 20 feet of rope tied in a loop)
] Ready the game by putting the rope around the ankles of two
children. They stand far enough apart that the rope becomes
taut, and it should be ankle-height above the ground. Feet in
the ropes are shoulder-distance apart. The jumper--the third
person--stands next to the rope, ready to jump in. You
should take on the role of the jumper.
2 Jump in the rope with both feet. Jump out of the rope, one
foot straddling each outside rope. Jump to the side of the
rope, straddling one side of the rope. Then straddle the other
side of the rope. Then jump on the rope, one foot on each
side. Then out again. Then take the rope. Cross it with the
feet, so that the feet are inside of an X. Jump out and
straddle the rope. With each jump, the two kids holding the
rope call out: "in, out, side, side, on, in, out.”
3 Jumper is out if she lands on the rope when she's not
supposed to, or if she is supposed to land on the rope and
misses. If the jumper gets through the routine without
missing, the rope is moved up the legs and the jumper gets to
try again at this bigher and harder level. With each success,
the rope gets moved higher and higher up: the knees, the
waist, under the arms, up to the neck.
4 Try a shorter trick variation. Start with both feet inside the
rope. Jump, putting both feet outside the rope. Jump with
both feet back in. Jump with one foot landing on the rope
and the other jumping outside of it. Jump so both feet land
on the rope. Move the rope up the legs of the other players

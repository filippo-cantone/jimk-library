## Reading is Thinking

```
STRATEGIES        MODEL         GUIDED PRACTICE     INDEPENDENT USE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Prior Knowledge → Read Aloud   → Shared Rdg        → Recip. Tchg
Visualising     → Read Aloud   → Shared Rdg        → Recip. Tchg
Questioning     → Read Aloud   → Shared Rdg        → Recip. Tchg
Inferring       → Read Aloud   → Shared Rdg        → Recip. Tchg
Det. Important  → Read Aloud   → Shared Rdg        → Recip. Tchg
  Ideas
Synthesising    → Read Aloud   → Shared Rdg        → Reciprocal
                                                  Teaching
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TC  → TO  → WITH  → BY        ←  Reading is Thinking
```

### What the columns mean

| Column | What happens here |
|---|---|
| **STRATEGIES** | The six comprehension strategies in sequence: Prior Knowledge, Visualising, Questioning, Inferring, Determining Important Ideas, Synthesising. |
| **MODEL** | "Read Aloud" — the teacher models the strategy by reading aloud. The strategy is made visible before students practise it. |
| **GUIDED PRACTICE** | "Shared Rdg" — shared/guided reading where teacher and students work the strategy together. |
| **INDEPENDENT USE** | Students take the strategy solo. Early strategies move through "Recip. Tchg" (Reciprocal Teaching); Synthesising finishes at "Reciprocal Teaching" as the capstone. |

The arrows show the progression: each strategy starts at **Read Aloud** (teacher model), moves into **Shared Reading** (guided practice), then into **Reciprocal Teaching** or independent use.

### The four stages at the bottom — TC · TO · WITH · BY

At the foot of the chart the abbreviations run across:

```
TC  →  TO  →  WITH  →  BY
```

These are Jim K's shorthand for the four stages of "Reading is Thinking" — the
release-of-responsibility model that the whole chart is built on. The full
expansion (written across the bottom axis of the original chart) is:

- **TC** — Think *to* the child (teacher models the thinking)
- **TO** — Think *with* the child (shared / guided)
- **WITH** — Think *with* the child, moving toward independence (guided → shared)
- **BY** — Think *by* the child (independent)

That is the backbone of the whole framework: the six strategies each travel the
same TC → TO → WITH → BY arc, and the chart is the map of how that happens
across the curriculum.

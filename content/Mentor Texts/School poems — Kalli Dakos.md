# School poems

*Kalli Dakos*

At the end of the day,

I start to sing,

Five seconds,

Four seconds,

Three seconds,

Two seconds,

One second,

Then I

**rrrrrrrrrrrriiiiiiiiiiiinnnnnnngggg!**

**Gym**

My head is aching,

Bong! Bong! Bong!

The balls are bouncing

All day long.

I’m trying so hard

To be strong.

My head is aching,

Bong! Bong! Bong

## Monkey Bars

Rightside up,

And upside down,

Back and forth,

And all around,

The kids

Are making monkey sounds. **by Kalli Dakos**

# A Little Pigeon Toad

*Fred Gwynne*

Mummy says Daddy is a little pigeon toad.

Grandma says our four bears came from Scotland.

Daddy says tennis rackets should be taught …

… and you can teach a dog to heal.

My teacher says I get along well with my piers.

Mummy says trees have knots …

… and flowers have pistols.

Daddy says city streets have man holes.

Mommy says there are naval oranges.

Mummy says her coat is a little thread bear.

Daddy knows a man who fished for a giant hammerhead.

Daddy says Mummy is in bed with hives.

Daddy says he wants a pool table.

After dinner Mummy and Daddy are going to show their slides.

In Sunday School they say when you are bad you should do pennants.

Daddy says he pitched a tent.

Mummy says she is going to a bridle shower.

Uncle Walter says he sews his fields…

…and lives off the land.

Daddy says I’m his air.

Daddy says there are three feet in a yard.

Yarns like these are hard to swallow.

# Shortcut

*Donald Crews*

We looked….

We listened….

We decided to take the shortcut home.

We should have taken the road. But it was late, and it was getting dark,
so we started down the track.

WHOO

We knew when the passenger trains passed. But the freight trains didn’t
run on schedule. They might come at any time. We should have taken the
road.

WHOO-WHOO

The track ran along a mound. Its steep sloped were covered with briers.
There was water at the bottom, surely full of snakes. We laughed. We
shouted. We sang. We tussled. We threw stones. We passed the cut-off
that led back to the road.

WHOO-WHOO

“I HEAR A TRAIN!”

Everybody stopped. Everybody listened. We all heard the train whistle.
Should we run ahead to the path home or back to the cut-off?

WHOO

The train whistle was much louder.

“Back to the cut-off!” “RUN”

WHOO

“THE TRAIN! THE TRAIN!”

“GET OFF! GET OFF!”

“GET OFF THE TRACKS!”

WHOO-WHOO

We jumped off the tracks onto the steep slope. We didn’t think about the
briars or the snakes.

KLAKITY-KLAK-KLAK-KLAK-KLAKITY-KLAKITY-KLAKITY-KLAKITY-KLAK-KLAK-KLAKITY-KLAK-KLAKITY-KLAKITY-KLAKITY-KLAK-KLAK

The train passed. We were all fine. We climbed back onto the tracks. We
hurried to the cut-off and onto the road.

WHOO-WHOO

We walked home without a word. We didn’t tell Bigmama. We didn’t tell
anyone. We didn’t talk about what happened for a very long time. And we
didn’t take the shortcut again.

> **Varying Examples of Organising Techniques**:
>
> Courage - B.Waber
>
> Uses different uses of the word courage. Students could choose another
> word to follow the example – *freedom. honor, justice.*
>
> The Z was Zapped – C.Van Allsburg
>
> The alphabet is the organiser. Action sentences for each letter.
>
> Switch on the Night – R.Bradbury
>
> Sophisticated organisation allowing for different teaching approaches.
> E.g. Repeated refrains.
>
> Story map leading to resolution.
>
> Compare and contrast beginning and ending.
>
> The Paperboy – D.Pilkey
>
> Transitions from dark to dawn without overt use of transition words.
>
> Rimshots – C.Smith
>
> Organising different genres on one theme. Students can use the model
> on subjects that matter to them. E.g. other sports.
>
> The Journey – S.Stewart
>
> Juxtaposition between Hannah’s diary entries of city discoveries, and
> pictures from her mind of life on the Amish farm.
>
> Dear Mrs LaRue: Letters from Obedience School – M.Teague
>

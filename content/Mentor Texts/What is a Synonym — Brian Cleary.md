# What is a Synonym? (Pitch and Throw, Grasp and Know)

*Brian Cleary*

**Pitch and Throw, Grasp and Know**

**What is a Synonym? Brian Cleary**

Do you feel tired or beat or exhausted from baking the cake that you’ve
baked and you’ve frosted?

Because there are synonyms, you get to choose

from a whole list of possible words you can use.

They’re words that are practically, nearly the same,

like, gentle, subdued, or incredibly tame.

Synonyms help us be less repetitious

by letting us choose between blue plates or dishes.

Celestial bodies are often called stars.

Streets can be avenues.

Autos are cars.

Synonyms help us to be more exact,

as in, our fridge was raided or stormed or attacked.

They give us more options and make things precise,

like nippy or freezing or chilly as ice.

They’re words like toss and pitch and throw,

comprehend and grasp and know.

Yell and holler.

Jump and leap.

Fly and soar,

and doze and sleep.

Richness and depth are what synonyms raise

when they’re used in a paragraph, sentence or phrase.

A lovely and pretty and beautiful city.

A cat or a feline could be called a kitty.

They let us pick showering, raining, or pouring.

Without them, our language would surely be boring.

You can choose between grateful or thankful, beholden

for the sneakers or tennis shoes, yellow or golden.

Don’t you agree that it’s pretty appealing,

we have all these choices to say how we’re feeling.

So now you’re a master! An expert! A whiz!

Because you know just what a synonym is!

So, what is a synonym?

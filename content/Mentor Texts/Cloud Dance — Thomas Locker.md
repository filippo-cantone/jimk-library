# Cloud Dance

*Thomas Locker*

Clouds of many shapes

and sizes drift across

the sky.

In every time and season

the colours of clouds

are always changing.

Brilliant sunset clouds

glow

and then fade.

Nightime clouds

with silver edges

shimmer in the moonlight.

High, wispy clouds

race

in the autumn wind.

Winter clouds,

low and thick,

bring sparkling snow.

Soft, rosy spring clouds

flow

in the dawn light.

Veils of mist

rise

in warm sunlight.

Delicate morning clouds

grow

and form.

Fluffy summer clouds

march

in the blue sky.

Grey clouds

with golden fringes

block the streaming sunlight.

Dark-and-light

storm clouds

tower.

Rain clouds,

full and heavy,

burst.

Water rising,

forming clouds of many

shapes and colours

that drift and dance

across the sky.

Good-night clouds.

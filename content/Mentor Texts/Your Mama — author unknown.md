# Your Mama

*Unknown*

Your mama was funny.

She could make just about anybody laugh.

She had red hair and freckles.

She like to plant things;

She had a talent for it.

She could stick a tyre in the ground

And grow a car.

She could run fast.

If you were racing her,

You couldn’t ever let her get a head start,

She would beat you for sure.

She couldn’t cook.

She burnt everything,

Including water.

She had a hard time opening a can of beans.

Your mama loved a story.

She would sit and listen to stories

All day long.

She loved to be told a story.

She especially liked funny ones,

Stories that made her laugh.

She knew all about the constellations,

Every planet in the nighttime sky,

Every last one of them.

She could name them

And point them out.

And she never got tired of looking up at them.

She hated being a preacher’s wife.

She said she just couldn’t stand having the ladies

At church judge what she was wearing

And what she was cooking

And how she was singing.

She said it made her feel

Like a bug under a microscope.

She drank.

She drank beer,

And whiskey,

And wine.

Sometimes, she couldn’t stop drinking.

And that made me and your mama

Fight quite a bit.

Your mama loved you.

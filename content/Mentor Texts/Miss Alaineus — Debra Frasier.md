# Miss Alaineus

*Debra Frasier*

## Book Summary

Sick at home with a cold, Sage makes a mistake with her spelling
homework with hilarious results. True to her Mum's comforting words,
however, "there's gold in every mistake." A clever, funny, and inviting
vocabulary tale.

Miss Take

“Mum,” I said, “I could only be a mistake this year. **Miss Stake.”**

How many other words are there that could trap Sage?

Talk about the mistake that Sage made with the word miscellaneous .

As a class, come up with a list of other words that start with mis -.

Choose one word to illustrate.

Mrs Page gives her class a vocabulary assignment. Sage is ill, so her
sentences about what she is doing has this as the theme, and is then
extended with her return to school.

*“Open the dictionary to the A section and write a sentence using three
words that begin with the letter A. Try to select words that are
different, unusual, or surprising to you. When you have completed your A
sentence, move on to the B section. Continue writing one sentence using
three words from each letter until you complete the alphabet. I’d like
your sentences to tell me something about your daily activities –
consider writing about what you are doing, thinking, or feeling. All
twenty-six sentences are due three weeks from today. Good luck, have
fun, and learn a lot of new words!”*

Sage’s assignment appears on an edge of each page.

A I have a feeling this awesome ailment will cause me great agony soon.

B What did I tell you? This berserk bacteria has bulldozed me badly.
Help!

C I croak when I cough, and I call this a terrible catastrophe.

D I am defective and delirious, and soon I will dwindle away.

E Can I endure this extraordinary redness in my eyeballs?

F This burning fever makes my future look dim and fuzzy.

G Can’t you hear my gloomy groans and gruesome wails?

H Won’t this heap of homework make my headache worse?

I If I don’t improve soon, insanity is inevitable.

J My bed is just a jumbo jumble of germy sheets.

K Did this ailment kick my years of knowledge back to kindergarten?

L Is it likely a bit of learning was lost each time I blew my nose?

M Did I mention that my moaning, mournful days are over?

N Did you see my turn is near and I’m not even nervous?

O Obliterate me, send me to oblivion –no one could outdo my stupidity.

P This is positively the most painful day of my short, pathetic life.

Q With one quiet question my life sank into quicksand.

R On this ruined, rotten day, it rains in my heart.

S Will I feel better someday, somewhere, somehow?

T Tell me this terrible twist of fate will end soon!

U Undaunted, I undertook those unforgettable steps onto the stage.

V The stage was vast, I felt valiant, and no one threw a vegetable.

W We waved and whistled and wished it would never end.

X If X = happy, then we were all X x 10 and chiming like xylophones.

Y Over yonder you’ll see me jump and yell with glee!

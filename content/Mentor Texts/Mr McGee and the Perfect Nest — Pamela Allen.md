# Mr McGee and the Perfect Nest

*Pamela Allen*

Lazy bird wanted a nest,

Nothing would do except the best.

“Is this the perfect nest?” said she.

“The nest that is right,

just right for me?”

“No-o-o!”

“This nest is too small.

It won’t do at all.

This isn’t the nest that is

just right for me.”

“Is this the perfect nest?” said she.

“The nest that is right,

just right for me?”

“No-o-o!”

“This nest is too big,

just right for a pig!

This isn’t the nest that is

just right for me.”

“Is this the perfect nest?” said she.

“The nest that is right,

just right for me?”

“No-o-o!”

“This nest is too tall.

See, it’s going to fall!

This isn’t the nest that is

just right for me.”

“Is this the perfect nest?” said she.

“The nest that is right,

just right for me?”

Hsssssssssssss …

Hsssssssssssss …

Hsssssssssssss …

OH!

OH! … OH! … AH!

Hsssssssssssss …

AHHHHHHH!

EEEEEEEH!

OWWWWWW!

OHHHHHH!

“Yes-s-s-s-s!”

“This ia the perfect nest,” cried she.

“The nest that is right,

Just right for me.”

# Fat Ferdie

*Pamela Allen*

Fat Ferdie – what a ferocious beast.

He likes nothing better than a feast.

Do you know what he likes to eat?

Little creatures! Red raw meat!

He’ll eat any creature he can find …

fur or feathers, he doesn’t mind.

Because Fat Ferdie ate so many

there came a time when there weren’t any.

So that same day he donned his coat

and climbed into his little boat.

He sailed away … to a distant land.

He pulled his boat up on the sand,

then off he went to find his dinner,

before he shrank and got much thinner.

Now just by chance there passed that way

three children who were out to play.

Although he’d never seen a child,

his stomach rumbled, and he smiled.

He gave a shout and started running.

The startled children heard him coming!

They dropped their toys and left their play.

As fast as they could they ran away.

Fat Ferdie was running very fast.

The smallest boy was coming last!

When – whoosh! –

up he went, into the air.

Fat Ferdie couldn’t reach him there.

Fat Ferdie couldn’t climb the tree.

(He was fat too fat, you see.)

He sat down on the ground instead.

THEN! something hit him on his head.

Angry now, he shook the tree.

Down came the children, one two three.

But lots of pears came down as well

and hit Fat Ferdie as they fell.

Dazed, he looked around then said,

“Where’s my dinner? I’ve not been fed!”

But the children didn’t stay;

they all ran home. They’d run away.

There were no other creatures there.

What could Fat Ferdie eat? A pear?

Lots of pears were going to waste.

Fat Ferdie thought, “I’ll have a taste.”

He took one bite and it was YUMMY.

He ate them AA and filled his tummy.

Now, what does Fat Ferdie like to eat?

Only pears, NEVER meat!

# In the Tall, Tall Grass

*Denise Fleming*

**In the Tall, Tall Grass Denise Fleming**

In the tall, tall grass …

crunch, munch, caterpillars lunch

dart, dip, hummingbirds sip

strum, drum, bees hum

crack, snap, wings flap

pull tug, ants lug

slip, slide, snakes glide

ritch, ratch, moles scratch

skitter, scurry, beetles hurry

zip, zap, tongues snap

hip, hop, ears flop

stop, go, fireflies glow

lunge, loop, bats swoop.

Stars bright, moonlight …

good night,

tall, tall grass.

# Bunny Cakes

*Rosemary Wells*

\\

Reflecting

**Bunny Cakes Rosemary Wells**

**It was Grandma’s birthday.**

**Max made her an earthworm birthday cake.**

**“No, Max,” said Max’s sister, Ruby. “We are going to make Grandma an
angel surprise cake with raspberry-fluff icing.”**

**Max wanted to help.**

**“Don’t touch anything, Max,” said Ruby.**

**But it was too late.**

**Ruby sent Max to the store with a list that said: EGGS**

**Max wanted Red-Hot Marshmallow Squirters for his earthworm cake. So he
wrote “Red-Hot Marshmallow Squirters” on the list.**

**EGGS**

**The grocer could not read Max’s writing.**

**“Eggs!” said the grocer, and he gave Max eggs.**

**Max brought the eggs home to Ruby.**

**“Don’t bump the table, Max!” said Ruby.**

**But it was too late.**

**Ruby sent Max back to the store with a list that said: MILK**

**This time Max wrote “Red-Hot Marshmallow Squirters” in a different
way.**

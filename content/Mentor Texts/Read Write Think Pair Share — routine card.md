# Read Write Think Pair Share

A classroom routine for turning a reading into paired talk and written synthesis.

**Read** — students read a short text (or a section of a longer one).

**Write** — each student writes down what they noticed: a key idea, a question, a connection, a word that matters.

**Think** — a moment of quiet to sort what they have written. What is the one thing to hold onto?

**Pair** — partners share what they wrote and compare. Where do their notes agree? Where do they differ?

**Share** — pairs report back to the class; the teacher collects the most useful ideas on the board.

Use it after a read-aloud, after independent reading, or as a bridge into a writing task. It works with any text type and any age, as long as the reading is short enough to hold in mind.

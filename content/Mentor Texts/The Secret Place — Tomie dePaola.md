# The Secret Place

*Tomie dePaola*

**The Secret Place**

**Tomie dePaola**

It was my secret place –

down at the foot

of my bed –

under the covers.

It was very white.

I went there

with a book, a flashlight,

and the special pencil

that my grandfather gave me.

To read –

and to draw pictures

on all that white.

It was my secret place

for about a week –

Until my mother came

to change the sheets.

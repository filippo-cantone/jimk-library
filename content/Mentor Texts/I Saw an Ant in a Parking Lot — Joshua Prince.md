# I Saw an Ant in a Parking Lot

*Joshua Prince*

**I Saw an Ant in a Parking Lot**

**Joshua Prince**

Well, I saw ant in a parking lot.

The sun was high,

the pavement hot.

He was walking along, not caring a jot,

a little black ant in a big black plot.

Now an ant’s objective in a lot

is not to park

but to find a spot

of sticky soda, crumbs, or what

some careless kids or crows forgot.

How do I know? I’m Dorothy Mott –

the ticket matron

of the lot.

I gather tickets, punch the clock,

and help lost parkers find their spots.

And on this morning (August hot),

in my booth

(upon the lot),

I sat with doughnut, coffeepot,

tickets ready, parkers sought.

When in came rolling to my lot

a mum, two kids,

and tiny tot,

all safely-belted in a squat

red minivan, prepared to shop.

Now minivans are surely not

a mini-peril

when you’ve got

a mini-ant out on a trot

across a mega parking lot.

My stomach turned into a knot.

Was this to be

his sorry lot?

To vanish in a tarry spot?

Ant under wheel? A van on top?

The van pressed on – our ant was caught

‘tween rolling steel

and pavement hot,

Between a mum, her van, her tot,

his trot, her turn, their fate, a spot …

O faster ant! O tire not!

O tyre turn

unless you blot

the living, breathing life out of

a hungry ant in danger caught!

Though desperate was I, and distraught,

I seized upon

a powdered thought:

A doughnut thrown across a lot

could also be …

a *warning* shot.

*Now* you’re thinking! Nice one, Dot!

You’ve hatched a plan,

but dally not.

The wheels are turning (so’s the plot).

Give doughnut wings – it’s worth a shot!

And so I summoned all I’ve got

to hurl a powdered

jelly-knot

above the lanes at just the spot

across that steaming parking lot.

Skyward … vanward … antward … SPLOT!

That sticky missile

hit the spot

just east of anguish, west of caught,

north of trouble, south of shot.

The ant gave chase! The van did not!

Braking fast,

it came to stop

with screeching tyres, squeals from tot,

just inches from ant’s breakfast spot.

Score: Dorothy 1, disaster naught!

An ant alive,

a van in spot!

A doughnut shared and coffee hot.

All is well in Dorothy’s lot.

So we had our breakfast,

ant

and

Dot.

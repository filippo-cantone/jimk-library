# The Pig

*Marilyn Singer*

Farmer grunts, Crows can be so annoying

True, true

You can’t count on crows

They’re always tOYING

with friends and foes alike

Yet when it comes to employing

their cheeky beaks

No one’s better than

those back-scratching bug-snatching

itch-pleasing twitch-easing

swindlers

whose visit I’m enJOYING

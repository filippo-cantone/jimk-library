# The Game

*Unknown*

Clap! Clap!

Stomp! Stomp!

Swish! Swish!

This is the way we get through

Our games.

The crowd shouts,

”Yahoo!”

The ball soars through the air.

Then, bounce, bounce, bounce.

The audience holds its breath.

SWISH!

The ball goes in;

We win!

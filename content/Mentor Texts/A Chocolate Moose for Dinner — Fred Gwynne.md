# A Chocolate Moose for Dinner

*Fred Gwynne*

Mummy says she had a chocolate moose for dinner last night.

And after dinner she toasted Daddy.

It says on TV there’s a gorilla war.

Daddy says he has trees for all his shoes.

Daddy says lions pray on other animals.

Daddy says he hates the arms race.

Mummy says we need a new wing on the house, but Daddy says he’ll have to
sleep on it.

Daddy says there should be more car pools.

Mummy says her favourite painter is Dolly.

Mummy says there are aeroplane hangers.

Daddy says he has the best fishing tackle.

It says on TV a man held up a bank.

He spent two tears in the pen.

And he has just escaped and is now on the lamb.

At the ocean Daddy says watch out for the under toe.

Daddy says he plays the piano by ear.

Daddy says that in college people row in shells.

And some row in a single skull.

Mummy says after she and Daddy argue they always kiss and make up.

Mummy says she’s going to tell me about Santa Claws.

And Daddy says he’s going to tell me the story about the tortoise and
the hair.

Stories like these drive me up the wall.

# Goal

*Robert Burleigh*

Score tied.

Muscles tense.

Ball drops.

After it – quick!

White spin-and swirl.

Feet like fins

in dark green water.

*Their ball! Get back!*

River-wide field.

Flail of knees.

Splash of many legs.

*Hold your position! Defence!*

Players backpedalling.

Digging heels.

Explosions of gouged grass.

*Mark tight! Guard the line!*

Crisscross. Flick.

And toe-tap quicker

than fingers opening.

*My side! Mine!*

Chip pass unspooling.

Boom off laces.

High as never-come-down.

*Watch their scorer!*

Corner kick curled and rising.

A pale rainbow.

Goaltender’s catlike leap.

*Great save! Clear it!*

One minute left.

Last-second charge.

All wheeling as one.

*Now! Make it happen!*

Upfield.

Many-eyed.

Alert to near and far.

*Push forward! Offence!*

Zig and zag.

Scrawl of leggy Z’s

on midfield air.

*Here! Through!*

Give and go.

Spinning inside.

Nearer and nearer.

*I want – I want it!*

Needle’s-eye chink

in a wall of wild bodies.

The far post.

*Launch it, striker!*

Blur of whiteness.

Past goalie’s single blink.

Suddenly, vibrating net.

*See! See! Is it?*

Leap for the sky.

Arms upraised.

Delirious dance.

*We did it! We did it!*

Goal.

*Goal.*

GOAL.

You, me, us –

champions.

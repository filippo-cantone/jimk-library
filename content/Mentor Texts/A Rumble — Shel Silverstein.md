# A Rumble

*Shel Silverstein*

**A Rumble**

They roar

Out of the river tunnels

Into the streaming streets,

As strong

As a pride of lions,

As long

As a gaggle of geese.

A rumble of trucks

Streaks through the city.

I’d like to drive one

Towering over taxis,

Diesels smoking!

I’d like to drive one,

Cars pulling over,

Cops waving!

I’d like to streak

Through the city

Part of

A rumble of trucks.

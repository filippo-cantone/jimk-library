# Sounds Spooky

*Christopher Cheng*

Whistling wind through the trees,

Squealing bats flying past,

Knocking noises on my window …

What’s that noise I can hear?

*Creeeeeeeeeeeeeeaaaak …*

I’m not scared.

Thunder rolling, tumbling, rumbling.

Lightning cracking, slapping, crashing.

Shed doors banging, smashing, slamming …

What’s that noise I can hear?

*CRAAAShh!*

I’m not scared.

Dogs whining, yelping, barking.

Cats miaowing, screeching, snarling.

Hooting, howling, honking, hissing …

What’s that noise I can hear?

*Plinketty Plunketty Plonk Plink Plunk Twang!*

I’m not scared.

On the roof, feet are running.

In the roof, there is scratching.

Somewhere close, there is tapping …

What’s that noise I can hear?

*Eeeeek!*

I’m not scared.

In my house, floorboards creaking.

In the hall, clocks are chiming.

From the toybox, there is mumbling …

What’s that noise I can hear?

*Clatter clang.*

I’m not scared.

Buzzing noises, closer, nearer.

Whispering voices, getting louder.

Thumping, clicking, whirring, whizzing …

What’s that noise I can hear?

I’m … not … scared!

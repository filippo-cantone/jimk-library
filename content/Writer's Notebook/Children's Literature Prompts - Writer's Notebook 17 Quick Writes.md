After reading [Hey, Little Ant]{.underline} students are paired as
ants/anteaters and write persuasive dialogues where the ant is trying to
persuade the anteater not to eat him and the anteater is trying to
persuade the ant that he must!  After modelling these, students select
another pair of animals (predator/prey) to create original persuasive
dialogues to perform for the class.

After reading this book, students think of an unusual animal that they
would like to have as a pet. They brainstorm ways their animal could be
useful/helpful and then write a persuasive letter to their parents to
convince them to let them have the pet.

![crayons
quit](assets/Children's Literature Prompts - Writer's Notebook 17 Quick Writes/media/media/image48.jpeg)

The Day the Crayons Quit by Drew Daywalt

Writing prompt: Think of an inanimate object that you spend a lot of
time with (for example, your backpack, your car, your desk, your shoes).
Write a letter from this object to you. What would it want you to know?
What would it complain about? What would it appreciate? How does it feel
about how you treat it?

![diary of a
worm](assets/Children's Literature Prompts - Writer's Notebook 17 Quick Writes/media/media/image49.jpeg)

Diary of a Worm by Doreen Cronin

Writing Prompt: Choose an animal or an insect and write a diary for a
week from that animal's viewpoint. Can be funny or informational.

![frog and
toad](assets/Children's Literature Prompts - Writer's Notebook 17 Quick Writes/media/media/image50.jpeg)

Frog and Toad Are Friends by Arnold Lobel

Writing Prompt: Think about the different personalities of Frog and
Toad. Then write a story about something they do together--watch a
movie, join a team, go swimming, form a band, whatever you like. In your
story, show how Frog and Toad react differently to this situation.

![Tuck
Everlasting](assets/Children's Literature Prompts - Writer's Notebook 17 Quick Writes/media/media/image51.jpeg)

If you had the choice, would you want to live forever? In Tuck
Everlasting, perhaps Natalie Babbitt\'s most famous book, ten-year-old
Winnie Foster faces that choice when she wanders into the woods and
comes across the Tuck family who has discovered the fountain of youth.
If you were Natalie, would you drink from the magic water of immortality
or refuse it?

![Phoebe\'s
Revolt](assets/Children's Literature Prompts - Writer's Notebook 17 Quick Writes/media/media/image52.jpeg)

What happens when you and your parents or friends don\'t agree about
something --- do you throw a fit or try to compromise? When
eight-year-old Phoebe gets tired of all the fancy clothes her mother
makes her wear (Phoebe\'s Revolt), she puts up a fuss and refuses to get
dressed for her party. She and her Mom eventually come to a compromise,
but only after her Dad gets involved. Tell us about a time you had to
make a compromise.

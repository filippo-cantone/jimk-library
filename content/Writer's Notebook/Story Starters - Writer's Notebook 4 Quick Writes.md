**Tell this story:**

As a Quick Write

There were fourteen potions on the shelf. Their labels were all missing.
She knew one of them was what she needed. She frantically glanced back
over her shoulder. There wasn't enough time to figure out which was
which. She grabbed the closest one and chugged it, hoping desperately
she'd picked the right potion \...

Developing

Make sure you establish the situation, introduce a narrator, and
organise the story that unfolds naturally while using dialogue and
descriptions of actions, thoughts, and feelings to develop experiences
and events. Provide a sense of closure at the end.

**Rip van Winkle**

**As the story goes, Rip Van Winkle fell asleep and woke up twenty years
later having missed out on some major changes in the world. Imagine this
really happened to someone and they just woke up, having missed the last
twenty years of life. Explain to them what's different about the world
and what they missed while they were asleep.**

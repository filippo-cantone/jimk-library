# Strategy Taxonomy — approved 2026-09-12 by Phill

Supersedes `IA-MAP.md`'s 8-shelf structure. Source of status: `MASTERPLAN.md`.
This is the site's primary navigation: two strategy branches (Reading,
Writing) plus two things that deliberately sit outside the strategy model
(Author Studies, Getting Started). Grounded in the corpus, not an abstract
literacy framework — built from Jim's own trait-tag vocabulary (Phase 2's
genre/trait tagging pass, 309 books) cross-checked against which course
packs actually exist.

## Three front doors

1. **Reading strategies** — browse
2. **Writing strategies** — browse
3. **Author studies** — browse (see below)

Plus **search by text**: find a specific book directly, see every
strategy/author page it's used on. Search and browse are two paths into the
same book pages — nothing lives only behind one door.
